# H3 Motion Translation Rules — English

## Why translation is necessary

Community-caption language is often short and categorical:

```text
doggy style
pounding
riding
close-up
POV
```

H3 performs better when the same intent is expanded into visible motion, spatial geometry, and temporal continuity.

## Translation dimensions

For every adult action, resolve:

### 1. Position
Examples:
- rear-entry / doggy style
- low kneeling rear-entry
- bent-over rear-entry
- missionary
- cowgirl
- reverse cowgirl
- seated straddle
- prone
- side-lying
- standing

### 2. Active subject
State exactly who drives the motion.

### 3. Active body part
Examples:
- pelvis / hips
- torso
- legs
- head and mouth for oral action

### 4. Direction
Examples:
- forward-and-back
- up-and-down
- circular / elliptical
- rocking

### 5. Frequency
Examples:
- slow
- moderate
- fast / rapid
- steady rhythmic cadence
- gradually increasing

### 6. Range
Examples:
- short-range
- moderate
- pronounced range of motion
- deep

### 7. Complete movement cycle
For thrusting, prefer:

```text
a clear backward reset followed by each forward drive
```

This prevents "fast" from degenerating into vibration-only motion.

### 8. Receiving-body response
Link at least two regions when strong action is requested:
- hips / waist
- shoulders / upper torso
- breasts / soft-tissue inertia
- head / hair secondary motion
- weight transfer

### 9. Stabilizers
Examples:
- both hands remain planted
- supporting knee remains planted
- raised leg remains elevated
- hand remains on hip / thigh / lower back

### 10. Camera
Use H3-native camera phrasing:
- medium close shot
- frontal three-quarter angle
- low-angle shot
- rear-view shot
- POV shot
- tracking shot
- static shot
- push in with small amplitude at slow speed
- arc shot

### 11. Continuity
Preserve:
- position
- identity
- anatomy/body proportions
- spatial relationship
- clothing state
- lighting/exposure
- face visibility when requested

## Common source → H3 rewrites

### Source
```text
pounding
```

### H3
```text
vigorous, continuous forward-and-back pelvic thrusts
at a fast, steady cadence with a pronounced range of motion
```

### Source
```text
riding
```

### H3
```text
the upper subject actively controls a repeated up-and-down riding cycle
through the hips and legs
```

### Source
```text
grinding
```

### H3
```text
close-contact pelvic movement following a small forward-back
or circular friction path
```

### Source
```text
bouncing / jiggling
```

### H3
```text
natural secondary soft-tissue motion caused by the repeated body movement
```

## Face-priority camera rewrite

When the user prioritizes the female lead's face:

```text
The camera maintains a frontal three-quarter medium close shot,
tracking with small amplitude while keeping <Subject 1>'s face
clearly visible. Her head remains lifted enough to stay within
the frame, and she occasionally directs her gaze toward the camera.
```

## Continuity block

```text
Maintain the same position, subject identities, body proportions,
spatial relationship, established clothing state, and lighting.
Avoid duplicated limbs, extra limbs, merged anatomy, sudden wide shots,
or unintended position changes.
```
